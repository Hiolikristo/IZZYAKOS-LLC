Receiving

PO context → physical arrival → Quality → label → reconciliation.

01

Resolve context

PO, QR or GS1 identifies the authorized receiving context and carries supplier, buyer/responsible, site, dates and line items forward.

02

Verify physical reality

Arrival, quantity, handling unit and mismatch remain distinct from the system expectation.

03

Route Quality

Inspection, HOLD/PASS/MRB and exception handling remain human/enterprise-authorized.

04

Label and reconcile

Physical verification, receipt history, attachments, print and requisitioner notification close the chain without inventing IFS success.

Tool Crib

Exact asset custody, not a generic checkout button.

SCAN OUT

Who took what, where and why?

Operator authority, borrower identity, workstation/mill, work reference, exact asset barcode, calibration/condition and return policy stay attached to custody.

SCAN IN

Return is not complete until condition is resolved.

The exact asset returns to INSPECTION PENDING, then a permitted role resolves PASS, REPAIR HOLD or DAMAGE HOLD.
