Role in the IZZYAKOS system

FastPath makes people legible. TRACEBridge makes work legible.

The shared discipline is evidence before action: identify who is authorized, what happened, what remains unresolved and which system owns the final transaction.

Authority

System-of-record respect

Enterprise platforms remain authoritative. TRACEBridge is an API-first oversight concept rather than an unauthorized replacement.

Evidence

Receiving, diagnostics, supervisor and tool-control journeys are modeled as explicit, reviewable workflows.

Resilience

Controlled state changes
