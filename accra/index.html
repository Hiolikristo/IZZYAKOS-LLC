Role in the IZZYAKOS system

The other layers explain the architecture. My Accra proves the field pressure.

A live grocery environment forces software to confront messy product identity, stock reality, customer communication, receiving, payments, pickup and staff permissions at the same time. That pressure is strategically useful across the whole company.

Customer layer

Storefront to pickup

Search, shop, cart, account, pickup, contact and loyalty journeys are treated as connected customer workflows rather than isolated pages.

Operations layer

Inventory to receiving

Products, stock state, receiving, orders, shelf locations and scan/pick workflows share a common operational model.

Authority layer

Owner and staff roles

Owner, manager, cashier, receiver, picker and viewer responsibilities are modeled with distinct permissions instead of one universal admin surface.

Merchant loop

Customer intent → inventory state → fulfillment → operational evidence.

This is where the IZZYAKOS thesis becomes tangible: evidence and workflow state have to survive the handoff from a customer screen to real shelves and real staff.

Customer side

Truthful commerce

  • Customer routes and CTA inventory audited
  • Pickup flow fails closed when required services are unavailable
  • Contact fallbacks route to real store channels
  • Payment language avoids simulated success
Owner side

Operational control

  • Receiving, inventory, orders and locations modeled
  • Product identity connects SKU, barcode and catalog reference
  • Inventory separates on-hand, reserved and available state
  • Owner operations include role and audit concepts
Truth boundary

The field case is real; the activation gates are also real.

The current release work records broad route-level audit coverage and a successful deployment for the owner-operations candidate. Production Supabase linkage, verified payment/webhook configuration, live customer checkout, staff login and physical catalog validation remain explicit before the merchant system is represented as fully live.
