Layer 03 · Movement · last-mile infrastructure

Make the true cost of movement visible.

CHOPX / ChopExpress tests a last-mile operating model where pickup, delivery and return burden are explicit; customer tips remain driver-owned; consent and compliance are recorded; and operational evidence can be reviewed instead of reconstructed after a dispute.
