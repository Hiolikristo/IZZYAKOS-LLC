Offer economics

Pickup + delivery + return-zone burden → transparent offer.

PPEM

Economic miles, not convenient miles.

The offer model reasons about the actual work pattern: travel to merchant, delivery leg and practical return burden.

Return-Zone Buffer

Dead-end routes have a cost.

The locked V1 $3 buffer is designed to recognize the burden of ending a delivery outside the practical work zone.

Tip Protection

Tips do not repair weak platform pay.

The customer tip is shown separately and remains driver-owned; it is not used to disguise inadequate base economics.

Driver protections

Fairness includes consent, compliance and operational truth.

Safe Decline + Consent Override

Acceptance must remain a real choice.

Accept, decline and override paths are explicit states so later review can reconstruct what was offered and how consent was captured.

Driver Compliance Gate

Eligibility has prerequisites.

Activation/compliance logic prevents production-like actions from bypassing required driver state.

Reserve Ledger

Plan for costs without pretending to hold money.

Maintenance, protection and tax-oriented reserve logic is planning/accounting support, not regulated custody or live banking.

Operational rules

Wait, closure and sealed-bag exceptions matter.

Locked V1 rules include the five-minute wait/store-closed/sealed-bag evidence path so field exceptions are not left to memory.

Evidence Vault

Delivery disputes should be reconstructable.

Field session

Bind events to the right driver/session.

Pickup, delivery, timing and exception events belong to an explicit field session rather than anonymous client state.

Evidence

Preserve what happened.

GPS/incident/receipt/drop-off concepts support later audit and dispute resolution.

Production gate

Durability still matters.

A private durable production Evidence Vault, retention policy and operational acceptance remain gates before claiming live evidence custody.
