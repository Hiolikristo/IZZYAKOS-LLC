One operating thesis. One current market-validation focus.

IZZYAKOS builds software for consequential real-world handoffs where identity, evidence, workflow state, authority or economic reality can become unclear. BOAMAN applies that discipline to people, capability and opportunity.

Current commercialization focus: BOAMAN. It is a deployed workforce-intelligence MVP entering structured customer discovery and controlled pilot validation. We distinguish what is built, what people report, what we observe, what remains a hypothesis, and what evidence validates or invalidates it.

Sprint objective

Prove the problem before scaling the product.

Determine which workforce problem is painful and frequent enough to fund, who owns it and its budget, which BOAMAN workflow matters most, and what measurable 90-day result justifies continuation.

Discovery target

10–15 documented interviews by Oct. 5.

Employer/recruiter, workforce/training/nonprofit and candidate interviews use past-behavior questions, not product-praise questions. Every interview gets a controlled evidence ID.

Outreach funnel

30 → 15 → 5 → 2–3.

Target 30 qualified prospects, 15 substantive conversations, five serious pilot discussions and two to three potential LOI/pilot commitments. These are sprint targets, not current traction claims.

Pilot offer

90-day controlled validation.

Measure intake, usable packets, qualified actions, referrals/interviews, partner workflow impact, trust, support burden, infrastructure cost and willingness to continue or pay.

The company thesis

Cohesion, not collection.

Separate applications, different users and deployment boundaries, one reusable control discipline.

01 · Identity

Who is acting?

Candidate, receiver, driver, merchant staff and owner roles are explicit rather than interchangeable.

02 · Evidence

What do we actually know?

Reported, uploaded, observed, verified and unresolved facts remain distinguishable.

03 · State

What has happened?

Requirements, receipts, deliveries and inventory events carry explicit state.

04 · Authority

Who may approve next?

Disclosure, enterprise actions, overrides and owner operations stay bounded by role authority.

05 · Economics

What does the action cost?

Training, movement, support, inventory and operational burden are made visible.

Company rule

Built is not validated.

Engineering completion, deployment, field use and market validation are different states.
