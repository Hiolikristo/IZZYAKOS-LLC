Operating principle

One venture, one reviewable story

Every public venture surface should answer four questions quickly: what problem exists, how the system works, what is proven now, and what the next validation milestone is.

  • No inflated traction language
  • No hidden reviewer dead ends
  • No feature list without a workflow
  • No pilot claim without pilot evidence
