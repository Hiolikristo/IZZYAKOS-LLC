How we prevent portfolio theater

Every layer has a truth boundary.

A reviewer should be able to tell what is code-complete, test-complete, deployment-complete, field-validated or still blocked. The company earns credibility by refusing to collapse those states into one word: “done.”

StateWhat it meansWhat it does not mean
BuiltThe workflow exists in code.It is not automatically field-proven.
TestedDeterministic acceptance/security checks are green.Synthetic tests are not customers.
DeployedA review build is reachable in its intended environment.Deployment is not adoption.
ValidatedA real user or operator has tested the problem/solution assumption.One validation event is not product-market fit.
