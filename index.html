Founder story

From learning by doing to building systems that make capability visible.

Nene Samuel Benjih Boama Medford founded IZZYAKOS LLC around a simple operating belief: Clarity → Cohesion → Efficiency.

The path

A multidisciplinary foundation.

Born in Ghana, Samuel's path has crossed computer hardware, radio programming and talent management, journalism, business administration, marketing and public relations, film and media production, music production, workforce training and industrial work. Much of his technology, software and systems-design practice has been self-taught and built through direct problem solving.

That nontraditional path exposed a recurring problem: people can have useful capability that conventional systems do not recognize, translate or connect to opportunity.

Why IZZYAKOS

Build what matters. Prove what works.

IZZYAKOS turns that lived insight into governed systems. BOAMAN is the current commercialization focus: candidate-controlled evidence connected to real job requirements, legitimate gaps, training, support and employer-readable outputs without reducing a person to an opaque score.

The broader company applies the same discipline to enterprise operations, logistics fairness, neighborhood commerce, community infrastructure and financial stewardship.

What we are seeking now

Validation, pilot partners and disciplined evidence.

We are preparing for structured customer discovery and controlled pilots with Central Ohio job seekers, employers, workforce organizations, training providers, community partners and sponsors. The goal is not to manufacture traction. It is to prove what works, document what does not, and earn the right to scale.

Company architecture

Truth before automation.

Across projects, automation cannot silently become authority.

Provenance

Know where a fact came from.

Candidate evidence, receiving events, delivery evidence and inventory state stay traceable.

Bounded authority

Humans and systems have explicit roles.

Hiring, receipt quantity, Quality disposition, driver consent and owner operations remain governed actions.

Fail closed

Unknown is not success.

Unresolved prerequisites stay visible instead of being converted into a green state for convenience.

Current company state

Reliable claims, explicit gates.

The portfolio is actively being hardened and validated. We do not represent pilots, integrations or revenue as complete until evidence supports them.
