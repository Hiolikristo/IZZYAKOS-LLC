Infrastructure systems for real-world work.

IZZYAKOS designs software around consequential handoffs: people to opportunity, physical work to enterprise records, drivers to sustainable delivery economics, and neighborhood commerce to reliable operations.

Company principle: identity, evidence, workflow state, human authority and economics should remain explicit. A system should never claim more certainty, completion or authorization than the underlying evidence supports.
