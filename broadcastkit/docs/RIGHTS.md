# Rights-state model

Catalog discovery is separate from permission to transmit.

- cleared: original/public-domain/CC/directly permitted use whose conditions are recorded.
- direct-permission: rights owner gave written permission.
- statutory/PRO-covered: station has documented coverage for the relevant use/territory.
- test-only: metadata may be scheduled privately; recording is not bundled or publicly streamed.
- blocked: insufficient evidence.

Store title, artist, source URL, license/permission, territory, allowed channels, attribution, reviewer and review date.
