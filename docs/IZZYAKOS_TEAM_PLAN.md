# IZZYAKOS LLC — Founding Team Plan
Status: Pre-seed / bootstrapped
Owner: Founder & Systems Architect
Purpose: Define the five-seat execution team around the founder and the first 90-day operating model.

## Founder retains
Company vision; systems architecture; product principles; strategic relationships; capital strategy; final prioritization; approval of major technical, commercial and governance decisions.

## Five team seats
1. Founding Product Engineer / Technical Delivery Lead
2. Program Operations Manager
3. Partnerships & Growth Lead
4. Product & Brand Experience Designer
5. Finance, Grants & Compliance Manager (fractional initially)

## Hiring sequence
Phase A: Engineer + Operations.
Phase B: Designer retained by project and Finance/Compliance fractional.
Phase C: Partnerships lead once product/operations capacity frees founder time and there is a defined pipeline.

## 90-day management cadence
- Monday: priorities, owners, dependencies, risk.
- Midweek: delivery check and blocker escalation.
- Friday: evidence review, accepted output, carry-forward.
- Monthly: access review, budget review, role-fit review.
- No project expands scope without an explicit owner, acceptance condition and resource decision.

## Candidate evidence
Use role-relevant work samples, references and a narrow evaluation assignment. Do not rely on resumes alone.

## Compensation principle
Classify the relationship correctly and document it before work begins. Equity, if later approved, is a separate compensatory instrument with vesting and legal/tax/securities review; it is not an informal substitute for wages that are legally required.

## Access model
Least privilege from day one. Repository, production, customer, financial and admin access are granted by role and revoked when no longer needed.

## 30/60-day conversion gate
At the end of the initial agreement evaluate: output quality, reliability, judgment, documentation, security discipline, collaboration, mission fit and affordability before renewing or expanding scope.
