# IZZYAKOS LLC — Founding Contributor Term Sheet Framework
Draft for discussion only. Not legal advice and not a substitute for a role-specific agreement reviewed by qualified counsel.

## 1. Parties and role
Identify IZZYAKOS LLC and the individual/entity. State the role, relationship classification and effective date.

## 2. Scope
List specific deliverables, excluded work, decision authority, reporting relationship and approval gates.

## 3. Term
Use an initial 30- or 60-day term with a written review/renewal point. No automatic promise of continued work.

## 4. Time and availability
State expected availability, meeting cadence and response expectations without contradicting the worker's lawful classification.

## 5. Compensation
State the actual cash compensation, project fee, retainer or other lawful compensation and payment schedule. Do not describe productive employee labor as "volunteer" work for a for-profit company. Any unpaid educational internship must separately satisfy applicable law.

## 6. Equity
No equity is earned or promised unless a separate written award/plan is approved. Any grant should specify instrument type, number/percentage or formula, vesting, cliff if any, treatment on termination, dilution, transfer restrictions, tax responsibility and required company approvals.

## 7. Expenses
Pre-approval threshold, receipts, reimbursement timing and prohibited expenditures.

## 8. Confidentiality
Define confidential information, exclusions, permitted use, security duties and survival after the engagement.

## 9. Intellectual property
Assign company-created work product to IZZYAKOS to the extent legally permitted. Identify pre-existing IP in a schedule and license only what the project needs.

## 10. Data and security
Least-privilege access; MFA; no credential sharing; approved devices/services; incident reporting; return/deletion of company data on exit.

## 11. Publicity and portfolio use
No public disclosure, logo use, customer naming, screenshots or portfolio publication without written approval.

## 12. Compliance
Follow company policies, applicable law, client restrictions and third-party license terms. Escalate legal, safety, privacy or security uncertainty.

## 13. Conflicts
Disclose competing engagements, conflicts of interest and third-party obligations that could affect the work.

## 14. Milestones and acceptance
Define acceptance criteria, review dates and who can accept/reject work.

## 15. Termination
State termination rights, final payment obligations, transition duties, return of property and access revocation.

## 16. No implied promises
No promise of funding, salary conversion, executive title, employment, equity, valuation, liquidity or future financing unless written in an authorized agreement.

## 17. Governing documents
Final agreement should identify governing law, dispute process and the complete agreement hierarchy after professional review.
