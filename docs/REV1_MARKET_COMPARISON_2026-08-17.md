# FastPath Market Comparison — Rev1 Customer Discovery Baseline

Date: 2026-08-17
Purpose: give Rev1 a credible competitive hypothesis without pretending the market has already validated FastPath's differentiation.

## Comparison frame

FastPath is not trying to win by claiming a more accurate secret score. The hypothesis is that workforce/employer operators and candidates may value a more transparent evidence workflow at the handoff between lived experience, a real role and the next action.

| Dimension | FastPath hypothesis | Eightfold platform-class comparator | Jobscan candidate-facing comparator | Current fragmented workflow |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Primary orientation | Candidate evidence → role requirement → action | Enterprise talent / skills intelligence | Resume → job / ATS optimization | Multiple disconnected tools |
| Evidence handling | Candidate-controlled, provenance-aware, confirmation before strong use | Skills/capability intelligence and inference | Resume and job-description analysis | Depends on operator/tool |
| Uncertainty model | Supported / Partial / Unknown / Gap | Talent/skills fit and potential intelligence | Match-rate / missing skill guidance | Often implicit/manual |
| Gap response | Evidence clarification, transferable proof, training/credential pathway | Talent development / opportunity matching | Resume optimization guidance | Separate referrals/manual follow-up |
| Disclosure boundary | Candidate reviews employer-facing output; continuity/support kept separate from merit | Enterprise talent workflow controls | Candidate resume/job-search workflow | Varies by tool/organization |
| Current maturity | MVP / pre-pilot | Mature enterprise platform | Mature candidate product | Existing incumbent process |

## What Rev1 customer discovery must test

1. Does an institutional buyer actually experience costly friction between intake, evidence, job requirements and referrals?
2. Is the pain large enough to create budget ownership, not just user interest?
3. Which buyer owns that budget: workforce organization, employer-facing workforce team, employer, sponsor or another actor?
4. Is explicit Unknown / provenance valuable, or does it add unacceptable workflow friction?
5. Does gap-to-action reduce unnecessary referral/training effort or improve operator confidence?
6. Is candidate-controlled disclosure important to buyer adoption or mainly to candidate trust?
7. Which existing tool/workaround would FastPath have to replace, integrate with or sit beside?
8. What measurable pilot outcome justifies payment?

## Claims rule

This document is a **competitive hypothesis**, not proof of superiority. No claim of higher hiring rates, better ATS passage, lower bias, faster placement, retention improvement or willingness to pay is valid until measured with real participants and buyers.
