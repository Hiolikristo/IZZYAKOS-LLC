# Startup Product

## 12. Brief product description

FastPath is a workforce-intelligence platform that helps a person compare candidate-controlled evidence against a specific job. It shows which requirements are **Supported, Partial, Unknown or Gap**, then routes unresolved requirements toward stronger evidence, training or other practical next actions. Candidates review what becomes employer-facing, while private continuity/support information remains separate from qualification merit.

## 13. Why this idea

The path from lived experience to a real job is fragmented. Work evidence may sit across resumes, documents, informal experience and training records while job requirements, training referrals and employment-support services live in separate systems. That fragmentation can make real capability difficult to see and can turn uncertainty into an apparent qualification failure.

FastPath tests whether one evidence-centered workflow can make capability more legible without inventing experience, hiding uncertainty or turning practical support conditions into negative hiring signals.

IZZYAKOS's other work reinforced the same systems lesson: consequential workflows become unreliable when identity, evidence, authority and workflow state are disconnected. FastPath is the single market-validation focus of this application.

## 14. Problem being solved

Workforce and employer-facing operators often connect candidate intake, a specific job's requirements, resume preparation, training referrals and practical employment supports across disconnected tools. Relevant experience can be under-described, missing evidence can be mistaken for missing capability, and people can be sent toward generic training instead of the specific unresolved requirement in front of them.

FastPath tests whether operators can move from candidate evidence to a job-specific, reviewable requirement map and legitimate next action in one workflow while keeping support conditions out of qualification merit.

## 15. How the solution works

The FastPath MVP accepts candidate-controlled work evidence and a target job, preserves provenance, and maps job requirements to Supported, Partial, Unknown or Gap. Unknown or missing evidence becomes an explicit follow-up rather than an invented fact. Gaps can route to evidence strengthening, transferable proof, training, credentials or apprenticeship pathways. Candidates review targeted outputs before disclosure.

The deeper application is a React/Vite web product with Supabase-backed persistence. Authentication, Row Level Security, source-freshness rules, automated regression tests and privacy boundaries are part of the MVP operating model.

For reviewer access, the company site now also contains an account-free fictional-data demonstration of the same mechanism. That demo is deliberately not presented as a production candidate record, customer adoption or a hiring prediction.

## 20. Biggest competitor / differentiation

**Closest platform-class comparator: Eightfold.** Candidate-facing tools such as Jobscan are also useful comparators for resume/job matching and ATS-oriented guidance. The practical incumbent is the fragmented combination of resume tools, ATS/job-search systems, spreadsheets/case management, training referrals and support systems.

FastPath's differentiation hypothesis is a candidate-controlled evidence workflow that:

- distinguishes Supported, Partial, Unknown and Gap rather than converting uncertainty directly into a match/potential score;
- preserves provenance and candidate confirmation before stronger employer-facing use;
- turns unresolved requirements into specific evidence/training/credential actions;
- separates private continuity/support conditions from qualification merit;
- lets the candidate review what becomes employer-facing.

Do not claim these differences are commercially decisive until customer discovery demonstrates buyer value.
