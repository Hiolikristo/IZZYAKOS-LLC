# Startup Product

## 12. Briefly describe your product/concept

FastPath is a workforce-intelligence platform that helps a person compare candidate-controlled evidence against a specific job. It shows which requirements are Supported, Partial, Unknown or Gap, then routes unresolved requirements toward stronger evidence, training or other practical next actions. Candidates review what becomes employer-facing; private continuity/support information remains separate from qualification merit.

## 13. Why did you pick this idea?

We chose FastPath because the path from lived experience to a real job is fragmented. A person's work evidence may sit across resumes, documents, informal experience and training records while job requirements, training referrals and employment-support services live in separate systems. That fragmentation can make real capability difficult to see and can turn uncertainty into an apparent qualification failure. We are testing whether one evidence-centered workflow can make capability more legible without inventing experience or hiding uncertainty.

IZZYAKOS also works on operational, movement and merchant systems. Those projects reinforced the same design lesson: consequential workflows become unreliable when identity, evidence and state are disconnected. FastPath is the single market-validation focus of this application.

## 14. What problem is the business solving?

Workforce and employer-facing operators often have to connect candidate intake, a specific job's requirements, resume preparation, training referrals and practical employment supports across disconnected tools. Relevant experience can be under-described, missing evidence can be mistaken for missing capability, and people can be sent toward generic training instead of the specific unresolved requirement in front of them.

FastPath is testing whether an operator can move from candidate evidence to a job-specific, reviewable requirement map and legitimate next action in one workflow while keeping support conditions out of qualification merit.

## 15. How does your idea solve the problem?

The FastPath MVP accepts candidate-controlled work evidence and a real target job, preserves evidence provenance, and maps each job requirement to Supported, Partial, Unknown or Gap. Unknown or missing evidence becomes an explicit follow-up rather than an invented fact. Gaps can route to evidence strengthening, transferable proof, training, credential or apprenticeship pathways. Candidates review targeted outputs before disclosure.

The current application is a React/Vite web product deployed with Vercel and Supabase-backed persistence. Authentication, Row Level Security, source-freshness rules, automated regression tests and privacy boundaries are part of the MVP operating model rather than future slideware.

## 20. Biggest competitor / what do you do better?

**Closest platform-class competitor: Eightfold.** It provides skills intelligence, talent matching and workforce-exchange capabilities for organizations. Candidate-facing tools such as Jobscan also compare resumes against jobs and recommend resume changes.

FastPath is deliberately narrower at this stage. Its differentiating hypothesis is a candidate-controlled evidence workflow that:

- distinguishes Supported, Partial, Unknown and Gap instead of converting uncertainty directly into a match/potential score;
- preserves provenance and candidate confirmation before stronger employer-facing use;
- turns unresolved requirements into specific evidence/training/credential actions;
- separates private continuity/support conditions from qualification merit;
- lets the candidate review what becomes employer-facing.

We do not yet claim that these differences are commercially decisive. Rev1 customer discovery should test which of them a real buyer values enough to adopt and fund.
