## Company thesis

IZZYAKOS is an infrastructure systems company. The four active applications are not four simultaneous go-to-market bets. They are controlled expressions of one operating discipline across four layers:

1. FastPath — people / workforce evidence and opportunity readiness
2. TRACEBridge — work / operational integrity and traceability
3. CHOPX / ChopExpress — movement / last-mile economic and evidence integrity
4. My Accra International Market — commerce / practical merchant execution

Shared primitives: identity and authority, evidence and provenance, explicit workflow state, auditability, economic guardrails, field validation.

## Focus rule

For this sprint, FastPath is the only Rev1-facing venture priority. The other three layers are supporting evidence for founder insight, reusable operating discipline and execution range. They are not presented as four companies seeking capital at once.

## Hostile rejection test

Reject the package if a reviewer can reasonably conclude any of the following:

- the company is a collection of unrelated prototypes;
- the primary customer or primary current venture is unclear;
- synthetic tests are represented as traction;
- a deployed build is represented as market validation;
- a product link requires an unexpected login;
- an application is represented as production-ready while its external activation gates remain open;
- private user, employee, driver or merchant information is exposed to prove the demo;
- one product's security or operating assumptions are copied into another without an explicit boundary;
- the company cannot explain how each layer contributes to a repeatable infrastructure thesis.

## Definition of done

A Rev1 reviewer can answer these questions in under five minutes:

1. What is IZZYAKOS? An infrastructure systems company.
2. What is the current venture being reviewed? FastPath.
3. Why do the other projects exist? They prove and refine shared infrastructure disciplines in adjacent real-world layers.
4. What is built now? Clearly separated by project and maturity state.
5. What is not built or not validated? Explicitly listed without euphemism.
6. What does IZZYAKOS want from Rev1? Venture-readiness guidance, market-validation discipline, customer/pilot introductions and a path toward scalable capital readiness.
