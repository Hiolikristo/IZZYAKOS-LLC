This demo checks identity and device readiness locally. Production will use a ministry-owned WebRTC room such as self-hosted Jitsi or another reviewed provider, then the producer brings approved guests into the program feed.

Production safeguards

Waiting room · host admission · display-name moderation · guest consent · role separation · kick/mute controls · recording disclosure · child/youth safeguards · no public meeting keys.
