FOUNDER · MINISTRY LEADER

Nana Agyemang

Born in Ghana and later led to the United States, Brother Agyemang's published ministry profile emphasizes prayer, deliverance, Scripture, spiritual renewal and counseling. He leads Jericho Hour Prayer Ministry with a focus on helping believers deepen their faith and recover purpose.

CO-LEADER · WOMAN OF GOD

Dunamis Abaawa Sarah

Born in Ghana and raised in Ivory Coast, Dunamis' published profile combines long professional experience with full-time ministry. She co-leads Jericho Hour with an emphasis on revival, prayer, holiness, teaching and family ministry.
