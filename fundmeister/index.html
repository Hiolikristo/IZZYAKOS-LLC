STANDALONE SYSTEM · IZZYAKOS IS THE FIRST TEST CASE
Project intelligence · capital readiness · stewardship · evidence

FUNDMEISTER remembers the whole journey — then shows what the project needs next.

FUNDMEISTER is designed as a source-agnostic operating system for founders, project teams, funders, sponsors and institutions. It can accept project information manually or from connected systems, track meetings and cohorts, organize relationships, identify talent/capital/equipment needs, build source-backed budgets, preserve evidence, and present a governed read-only view to authorized stakeholders.

FUNDMEISTER does not require GranTIFY. GranTIFY is one optional funding-intelligence source. FUNDMEISTER is the system that keeps the project context, decisions, evidence, needs, capital story and stewardship chain coherent regardless of where the data originated.

Standalone engine map

From first idea to accountable deployment.

These engines form the product architecture. Some are active in the IZZYAKOS control plane now; bank/payroll/provider integrations remain explicitly gated.

01 · Context

Project Intake Engine

Identity, legal owner, product/program type, stage, geography, launch target and operating assumptions.

02 · Memory

Journey/Event Ledger

Meetings, interviews, cohorts, applications, demos, networking, decisions, milestones and next actions.

03 · Relationships

CRM & Network Engine

Partners, funders, customers, advisors, institutions and relationship stages without forcing a specific external CRM.

04 · Capital

Funding Intake Engine

Grants, equity, loans, sponsorships, revenue, in-kind support and manual/API-supplied opportunities.

05 · Match

Opportunity Matching Engine

Match the actual project profile to opportunities and preserve why a match was made or rejected.

06 · Diligence

Eligibility & Evidence Engine

Requirements, source URLs, deadlines, restrictions, prerequisites, evidence gaps and verification state.

07 · People

Talent Needs Engine

What role is needed, why it is needed, capacity, skills, timing and funding dependency.

08 · Assets

Equipment Needs Engine

What equipment or infrastructure is required, acquisition model, quantity and source-backed cost basis.

09 · Finance

Sources & Uses Engine

Capital need, cost assumptions, approved budgets, restrictions, committed amounts and actuals.

10 · Reconcile

Transaction Engine

Authorized financial feeds, transfer reconciliation, project allocation, reimbursements and evidence state.

11 · Evidence

Provenance Vault

Receipts, quotes, contracts, reports and versioned evidence linked to the claim or transaction they support.

12 · Work

WECL / Payroll Bridge

Defensible human-work allocation from WECL; payroll remains authoritative for compensation and statutory records.

13 · Review

Professional Review Queue

Bookkeeping, CPA/tax, legal, securities, privacy, security, insurance and payroll questions routed to qualified reviewers.

14 · Validate

Pilot & Test Evidence

Tester sessions, scenarios, outcomes, issues, persistence/stability observations and acceptance evidence.

15 · Report

Investor/Funder Reporting

Milestones, sources and uses, variance, risks, evidence and next needs for authorized stakeholders.

16 · Learn

FUNDMEISTER Academy

Required founder/operator learning so capital stewardship and company building improve alongside the product.

Authorized transparency

An investor should be able to see the logic without seeing everything.

RBAC separates identity from authority. Access is scoped by organization, role and project.

Read-only stakeholder view

What an authorized funder can inspect

Project stage, milestones, validation evidence, capital need, talent need, equipment need, approved sources/uses, reporting obligations and visible risks — only for projects they are authorized to see.

Not automatically exposed

What stays protected

Bank credentials, private candidate records, payroll details, unrestricted evidence, unrelated company projects and administrative secrets do not become visible merely because somebody signed in.
