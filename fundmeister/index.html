FUNDMEISTER connects the funding story to the financial evidence.

Funding discovery is only the beginning. FUNDMEISTER is designed to carry a project from capital search and due diligence through approved use of funds, source-backed budgets, transaction reconciliation, evidence, reporting, WECL allocation and payroll integration.

Core rule: every material dollar should have provenance. An estimate stays an estimate until a source, quote, contract, transaction or approved record supports it.

Lifecycle

One chain from opportunity to evidence.

FUNDMEISTER is bookkeeping-adjacent, but its distinctive job is stewardship across the funding lifecycle.

Control architecture

Designed to fail closed.

Unknown data is not silently converted into revenue, compliance, a receipt, an allowable expense or an approved budget.

Provenance

Source before claim.

Funding sources, cost assumptions, evidence objects and transaction records retain source references and verification state.

RBAC

Authority is explicit.

Founder/admin, finance, project manager, bookkeeper, accountant, auditor and funder-readonly roles are separated.

Boundary

No credential vault in the ledger.

Bank and payroll provider secrets do not belong in financial tables. Provider credentials stay in an approved secrets/integration layer.

WECL

Human work stays human.

WECL can contribute defensible project-allocation evidence. AI/tool runtime is never represented as human labor.

Payroll

Integrate, do not duplicate.

Payroll systems remain authoritative for compensation and statutory records; FUNDMEISTER reconciles approved allocation.

Professional review

Software is not a CPA.

FUNDMEISTER can organize tax/accounting evidence and flag issues, but licensed judgments and filings remain with qualified professionals.

Internal-first deployment

IZZYAKOS is the first operating environment.

BOAMAN is the first pilot tenant. The same control plane is intended to bootstrap across CHOPX, TRACEBridge, My Accra, Pantryster and future IZZYAKOS projects.

Now

Control-plane foundation

Project registry, nine default workstreams per project, BOAMAN use-of-funds categories, planned corporate email registry, finance RBAC and read-only management views are persisted.

Gated

Real money movement

Bank connections, real transactions, receipts and payroll are not represented as active until provider authorization, write-path controls, evidence storage and security review are complete.
