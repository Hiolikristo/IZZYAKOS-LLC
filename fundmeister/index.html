FUNDMEISTER Academy

Capital literacy is part of stewardship.

The Academy is designed to improve founder/operator capability, not simply collect fees.

Curriculum

Eight required weekly foundations are already modeled.

Business model & operating system; founder finance/bookkeeping; funding/capital instruments; tax & CPA readiness; legal/fundraising boundaries; customer discovery/pilot design; investor/funder governance; cybersecurity/data stewardship.

Progression

Completion has consequences—but records are preserved.

Required courses can carry disclosed attendance/completion rules. The system tracks unexcused misses, warnings, pause state, catch-up evidence and human review instead of silently deleting an account.

Baseline policy: first miss → recovery task; second miss → warning and catch-up plan; third unexcused miss → Academy access may pause. Human review is required for disputes, accessibility, emergencies and ambiguous attendance evidence. Financial and audit records are not erased merely because Academy access pauses.
Platform & integration

Connect to the stack a company already uses.

FUNDMEISTER is intended to be API-first and cross-platform. The operating model separates provider authority from FUNDMEISTER stewardship context.

Inputs

Manual, API, CSV or provider adapters.

GranTIFY can supply funding intelligence, but CRM, accounting, payroll, bank, project-management and document systems can integrate independently.

Authority

Integrate, do not impersonate the source system.

Payroll remains authoritative for pay/tax records; accounting remains authoritative for books; banks remain authoritative for posted transactions.

Security

RBAC + project scope + audit.

Authentication is not authorization. Company membership, role, project scope and evidence classification determine what a user can see or change.

Internal-first proof

IZZYAKOS is the first operating environment.

BOAMAN and My Accra are the current priority operating cases. FUNDMEISTER is being used to organize their validation, talent, capital, equipment, professional-review and evidence requirements before broader commercialization.

Persisted now

Operational control-plane records exist.

Project tasks, milestones, talent needs, capital needs, equipment needs, professional reviews, workspace decisions and Academy courses are persisted. Validation, journey and relationship tables are ready for real evidence as it occurs.

Not claimed

Five completed BOAMAN tests are a target, not yet a claim. Real bank transactions, live payroll and grant awards remain inactive until evidence and security controls exist.
