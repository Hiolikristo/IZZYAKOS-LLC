Problem / solution

Repeated hiring is expensive when fit breaks down.

Employers repeatedly recruit, screen, hire, onboard and retrain. BOAMAN is testing whether better evidence of real skills and transferable experience can help identify stronger candidate-to-role fit before and after the hire.

The problem

High turnover and low retention create repeated recruiting, onboarding, training and productivity costs. At the same time, conventional screening can make real capability difficult to see.

The BOAMAN layer

BOAMAN sits before or alongside the ATS as an evidence layer: real experience → job requirements → Supported / Partial / Unknown / Gap → hiring/start → 30/60/90-day outcome signals where observable.

The validation question

Do employers and workforce systems experience this problem strongly enough to change workflow, run a bounded pilot and pay when fit or retention outcomes improve?

How BOAMAN works

Evidence → fit → support → retention.

The workflow makes uncertainty visible instead of hiding it inside a score, then follows agreed outcomes after the hiring decision.

1

Choose a real target

Start with a current job or documented occupational goal.

2

Build evidence

Add resume, work history, training, credentials and candidate-confirmed experience while preserving provenance.

3

Map requirements

Mark job requirements as Supported, Partial, Unknown or Gap. No hiring probability is produced.

4

Measure what happens next

Route legitimate gaps to training/support and, in a pilot, track starts and 30/60/90-day retention signals where the partner can observe them.

Who BOAMAN serves

One evidence record, several accountable users.

BOAMAN is designed to complement—not replace—the systems and professionals already responsible for hiring, staffing, training and workforce support.

Job seekers

Make capability visible, preserve evidence and understand the next legitimate step toward a target role.

Employers

Review clearer evidence against actual role requirements, test fit before hiring and measure whether the process improves early retention.

Staffing agencies

Reduce repeated reconstruction of candidate history and test which evidence makes screening more useful.

Workforce programs

Connect candidate evidence, referrals, training/support and employer outcomes across state and local workforce infrastructure.
