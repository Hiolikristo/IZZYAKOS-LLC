Problem / solution

People are more capable than the documents used to judge them.

Conventional resumes, applications and ATS workflows can lose useful context when experience is fragmented, informal, interrupted, foreign, reentry-related, self-directed or learned on the job.

The problem

Recruiters and workforce organizations often receive incomplete signals: titles, dates, keywords and credentials without enough structured evidence of what a person can actually do.

The BOAMAN layer

BOAMAN translates candidate-controlled evidence into employer-readable capability, separates supported evidence from unknowns and gaps, and connects the record to real job requirements.

The validation question

Which buyer experiences this problem strongly enough to change workflow, pilot the system and pay for the outcomes that matter to them?

How BOAMAN works

Evidence → alignment → next action.

The workflow is designed to make uncertainty visible instead of hiding it inside a score.

1

Choose a real target

Start with a current job or documented occupational goal.

2

Build evidence

Add resume, work history, training, credentials and candidate-confirmed experience while preserving provenance.

3

Map requirements

Mark job requirements as Supported, Partial, Unknown or Gap. No hiring probability is produced.

4

Close only real gaps

Route legitimate training, credential or evidence needs and produce a candidate-reviewed output.

Who BOAMAN serves

One evidence record, several accountable users.

BOAMAN is designed to complement—not replace—the systems and professionals already responsible for hiring, staffing, training and workforce support.

Job seekers

Make capability visible, preserve evidence and understand the next legitimate step toward a target role.

Employers

Review clearer evidence and gaps against actual role requirements while retaining hiring authority.

Staffing agencies

Reduce repeated reconstruction of candidate history and test which evidence makes screening more useful.

Workforce partners

Improve continuity across intake, referrals, training, reentry pathways and documented outcomes.
