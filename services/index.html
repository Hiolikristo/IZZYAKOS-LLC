Working method

Clear scope. Evidence. Acceptance.

1. Define the problem

Share the current workflow, users, deadline and constraints. We identify what can be inspected without exposing credentials or confidential data.

2. Agree scope & price

Receive a written task list, exclusions, review milestones and acceptance criteria. Quotes depend on complexity and access.

3. Deliver & verify

Review a demo or documented changes, test the agreed journeys, and receive a concise handoff. Production changes require permission and appropriate checks.
