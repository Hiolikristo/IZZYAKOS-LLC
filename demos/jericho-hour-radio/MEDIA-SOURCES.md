## Media ingest / production gate
Every track needs: stable ID; creator/composer/performer/master owner; original and license source URL; license type/version; attribution text; commercial broadcast permission; recording and composition rights; territory; streaming vs on-demand/podcast use; modification/derivative status; signed release; expiry/review; content reviewer, approval time and removal route. A Wikimedia free-content tag is a starting point, not a guarantee every territory/use is free.

For original Ghanaian gospel and highlife, prioritize signed non-exclusive written promotional broadcast agreements with Ghanaian musicians, the church choir and local artists. Producers should upload verified source masters rather than scrape streaming services. The public demo intentionally does not feature unlicensed commercial tracks.
