COMMUNITY MARKETPLACE

A place for every good idea.

Imagine a member-owned business directory where a caterer, barber, mechanic, designer, tutor or local shop can have a professional mini-storefront, share a short video and receive legitimate inquiries.

Businesses would opt in, submit verified information and control what is public. Ministry leaders would moderate listings; no storefront or endorsement is implied before review.
