AUTOMATION

How a day runs

  1. Approved music and station IDs play through scheduled AutoDJ rotation.
  2. The live presenter connects with a DJ-specific credential; AutoDJ fades under the show.
  3. Recordings are retained in protected storage; a producer reviews and approves replay/captions.
  4. Editorial clips are queued for approved platform accounts; a moderator reviews youth submissions.
OPERATIONS

Required before launch

  1. Owner approval, official logo, branding and verified handles.
  2. Approved weekly program schedule and designated booking agent.
  3. Broadcast VPS, backup account and music/sermon asset rights ledger.
  4. Authorized admin accounts with MFA, a qualified safeguarding lead and an incident protocol.
