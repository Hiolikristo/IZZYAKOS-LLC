# Jericho Hour — Programming, Product and Rollout Proposal
**Concept only, owner approval and calendar confirmation required.** 25 September 2026.

## Editorial premise
Keep the ministry's prayer, teaching, deliverance and worship mission at the center. Build a separately identified **community opportunity** branch to serve families, youth, entrepreneurs and people seeking homes or careers. The website becomes a single portal to the ministry's media and scheduling, not an unmoderated social network or a way to sell congregation data.

## Publishing rhythm
For each **approved** broadcast, publish one event record and landing page with local timezone, livestream destination, host and accessibility information. Record one source master, produce captions/transcript, then create approved 9:16 clips for YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels. Send opt-in WhatsApp announcements **linking to the approved item** (never copying private messages or group-membership lists). Facebook posts and Stories reuse platform-supported assets. Return comments to a staffed moderation queue.

## Booking agent workflow

1. Published session type, pre-requisites, number of people per session and owner-approved appointment availability.
2. Public request: only essential contact + type + selected slot, protected from content editors. Obtain informed consent and display privacy information before any personal intake.
3. Booking-agent console: triage request and logistics, send confirmation, provide video/in-person instructions, maintain a private status ledger; do not place confidential counseling notes in a broadly visible calendar.
4. Opt-in email/WhatsApp reminders, calendar rescheduling and accessibility accommodations. No marketing consent assumed from counseling requests.
5. After-session private administrative closure and retention controls. Real emergencies need qualified services, not an automated spiritual booking workflow.

## Member mini-stalls and partner referrals

Opt-in business verification includes name, category, general service area, contact method, approved photos/short videos, external commerce link and expiration/review date. Separate customer reviews from church endorsements. Flag commercial solicitations, prohibit sale of member contact lists and give each vendor a self-service update/removal route. Use simple external commerce/booking links first; add payments and local delivery only when appropriate agreements and taxes/compliance are in place.

Resource cards initially direct visitors to established official providers, including Homeport homebuyer education, OhioMeansJobs Columbus-Franklin County and ECDI business education. Workshops require separate partner invitations; published resource links do not imply partnerships or guaranteed eligibility. BOAMAN, ShopExpress and Pantryster remain **opt-in future integrations**, each with its own terms and separate tenant data.

## Safeguarding, data ownership and rights

- Children/teens: parental consent, background-screened youth leads as appropriate, review of every youth media upload, no private adult-to-minor messaging, guardian-controlled participation/removal, crisis and incident response plans.
- Prayer/counseling: encrypted private submissions, MFA for coordinators, role-level row security, minimal retention, private audit logs, explicit permissions for recording/testimonials, no analytics on disclosed sensitive details.
- Audio and media: use original or appropriately licensed music; public-performance and audiovisual rights may be distinct. Book appropriate rights before recording or syndicating live music across platforms.
- Ownership: the ministry owns its domain, branding, faith/program content and member data. Specify any IZZYAKOS reusable software IP and support fee in a clear service agreement; do not share member records with IZZYAKOS's other products absent express consent and proper agreements.
- Accessibility: mobile-first, captions, large touch targets, color contrast, keyboard and screen-reader testing, language strategy set by the ministry.

## Delivery / estimates for planning only

- **Pilot preview (built in this package):** single-page UI and offline-capable installable starter. No deployment, true social feed, push or appointment backend yet.
- **Phase 1, about 1–2 weeks once assets/access arrive:** approved content and logo; existing booking integration; schedule CMS; mobile QA; public preview and owner sign-off.
- **Phase 2, about 2–4 further weeks:** staff login, booking coordination, opt-in notifications, YouTube API, accepted social embeds, moderation and admin audit trails.
- **Phase 3, about 4–8 further weeks:** business listings, recorded programming workflow, workshop signups, teen-broadcast safeguarding and vetted resource referrals.

The quoted durations are engineering planning ranges, not commitments. Cost and scheduling depend on original CMS, actual social APIs, authorized access and agreed feature scope. Prefer the existing YouTube/video and booking platforms over building expensive new video hosting or a payment processor prematurely.

## Practical release metrics

Watch: unique visitors to official channel, replay click-through, event registrants and actual attendance. Booking: requests vs confirmed slots, cancellations, first-response time without storing private session details in analytics. Community: opt-in directory listings verified, resource link conversions (aggregate), webinar registrations and completed workshops. Youth programming: consent and moderation compliance before publication. Privacy: opt-in notification rates and deletion-request processing.
