How it works

A transparent evidence loop.

FastPath does not claim to predict hiring or ATS passage. It explains the relationship between a target job and the evidence a candidate has chosen to provide.

Target

Start with a real role so requirements, location, compensation and source context are specific.

Confirm

Preserve provenance and keep candidate-reported information distinct from independently verified facts.

Align

Map each requirement to Supported, Partial, Unknown or Gap and show why that state was assigned.

Gap → action

Resolve what can be resolved.

An unresolved requirement becomes a specific next action: clarify evidence, strengthen transferable proof, or route to training, credential or support resources.

Candidate control

Separate working record from disclosure.

Private continuity, support and working information is not automatically converted into employer-facing content. The candidate reviews outputs before use.
