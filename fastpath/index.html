Role in the IZZYAKOS system

People are the first infrastructure layer.

FastPath applies the same IZZYAKOS discipline used across operations, logistics and commerce: identity, evidence, state and authority must be explicit before a system makes a consequential recommendation.

Evidence

Candidate control

Working records remain distinct from employer-facing outputs, and reported information remains distinct from independent verification.

Action

Close what can close

How it works

A transparent evidence loop.

FastPath does not claim to predict hiring or ATS passage. It explains the relationship between a target job and the evidence a candidate has chosen to provide.

Target

Start with a real role so requirements, location, compensation and source context are specific.

Confirm

Preserve provenance and keep candidate-reported information distinct from independently verified facts.

Align

Map each requirement to Supported, Partial, Unknown or Gap and show why.
