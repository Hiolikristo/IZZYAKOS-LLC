## Release acceptance

- 12 HTML entry points and 10 dynamically rendered proposed program profiles.
- Browser-local program editor, import/export, launch checklist and draft social handles; no real authenticated backend.
- Demo donation cart is local only; no payment processing or donor data storage.
- Proprietary owner-supplied branding: use only in the agreed ministry project.
- Original demo audio only. No older commercial Ghanaian music or extracted sermon audio is bundled absent rights/owner permission.
- 32 kbps theoretical raw stream ~14.4 MB/listener-hour; actual egress depends on transport and listener count.
- IZZYAKOS is vendor/operator in the proposal, **not** a public-facing network of cross-linked products before the ministry's approval and adoption.
